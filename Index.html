How I Think About Campaigns

A systematic, disciplined approach that prioritizes real business margin and verified buyer intent over vanity metrics.

Where traffic and leads come from

We identify where your buyers are already demonstrating intent. Instead of spraying budget, we capture active commercial queries through Google Search and create message-market resonance on Meta through hook-tested creative formats.

  • ✓ High-intent search keyword isolation
  • ✓ Angle-driven paid social creative hooks

2. Qualification

How poor-fit leads are filtered

Cheap leads that waste sales time are expensive in reality. We insert intentional friction into copy, budget parameters, and form fields to ensure sales reps talk only to prospects with valid intent, verified contact info, and adequate budget.

  • ✓ Negative keyword placement scrubbing
  • ✓ Pre-qualifying copy & price thresholds
  • ✓ Automated form validation rules
03 trending_up

3. Optimization

Continuous feedback & refinement

Campaigns are living software. We analyze GA4 multi-touch paths, study creative decay curves, review landing page drop-offs, and re-allocate spend toward ad sets and search queries that generate actual closed revenue.

  • ✓ Iterative creative testing matrix
  • ✓ CRO feedback & post-click speed
  • ✓ Clean GA4 event attribution models
Growth Philosophy

Things I think about growth.

Observations collected from managing live budgets, debugging conversion tags, and scaling competitive search share.

Lead Quality 4 min read

Why lead quality matters more than lead volume

Low CPL metrics look great in weekly reporting until the sales team rejects 80% of contacts. True efficiency means inserting qualification friction at the ad level to protect sales bandwidth.

CAC • Qualification • CRM
Paid Social 5 min read

How I approach Meta Ads optimization

Audience targeting is largely automated by machine learning today. Your creative angle IS your targeting. Hook rate, hold rate, and post-click messaging continuity determine cost per conversion.

Creative Testing • Meta • Funnels
Organic Strategy 4 min read

SEO is more than ranking keywords

A #1 rank for a non-transactional vanity term yields zero revenue. Real organic strategy maps search intent tiers against product monetization levers and technical crawl health.

Intent Clusters • Tech SEO • CRO

What GA4 tells you that ad dashboards don't

Ad managers over-credit themselves using proprietary view-through attribution models. GA4 cross-channel pathing reveals real drop-offs, multi-touch assists, and post-session churn.
